ARGUS NAVIGATION SYSTEM
FLIGHT + HUD LEGEND
This screen explains how to move through the evidence field and operate the HUD. Navigation changes only what you are looking at. It does not change qualification, authority, paper-trading rules, or evidence.

TOUCH / iPAD / PHONE

DRAG FIELD
Rotate / look around the 3D field.
MOVE PAD
Fly forward, backward, and sideways through the evidence volume.
PINCH
Zoom toward or away from the field.
TWIST
Roll the camera.
TAP NODE
Inspect a symbol or pattern.
DOUBLE TAP NODE
Center the camera on that object.
DOUBLE TAP EMPTY
Return the current view to home.
+ / − PAD
Dedicated zoom controls. ↑ / ↓ changes vertical position and ↶ / ↷ rolls the view.

DRAG

HUD MAP

LB · TARGET
Filter what is visible by evidence class, firing frequency, directional accuracy, expected net, asymmetry, and move magnitude.
RB · VIEW
Change projection: Universe, 3D Graph, Risk Cube, Frontier, Orbital, Donut, Funnel, Sphere, Torus, Helix, Pyramid, Cylinder, Black Hole, plus render/color/scale channels.
Y · FIELD
Change visual layout, Fusion / Shadow / side-by-side mode, reality embedding, physics, tools, and connection display. Shadow Market is an inferred low-density display layer; these controls do not alter trading authority.
HIST / ROLL / BOTH
Choose which evidence universe is displayed.
‹ BACK
Leave a drilled-in sector / universe / navigation level.
⌂ HOME
Return to the default Observatory field.

HOW TO EXPLORE

1 · TARGETNarrow the universe only if you want to hunt a specific behavior.
2 · VIEWChoose the geometry that makes that behavior easiest to see.
3 · FLYMove through clusters, sectors, risk surfaces, or evidence layers.
4 · INSPECTTap a symbol for price, signal, evidence, risk, and paper-trade detail.
Important: VIEW and FIELD are lenses. TARGET is a display filter. None of them promote a candidate, rewrite the 694 historical authority rows, or change paper-trading eligibility.
